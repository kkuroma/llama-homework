Characters:
    - Lord Ruthven: A nobleman with a deadly hue and a mysterious aura, he instills fear and awe in those around him. He is enigmatic, often engaging with the virtuous while showing a preference for the corrupt.
    - Aubrey: A young, wealthy gentleman with a romanticized view of life, he is naive and idealistic, believing in the inherent goodness of people. He becomes fascinated with Lord Ruthven and seeks to understand him.
    - Ianthe: A beautiful and innocent Greek girl who captivates Aubrey with her charm and stories, particularly about supernatural beings like vampires.
    - Miss Aubrey: Aubrey's younger sister, who possesses a melancholy charm and becomes a source of comfort for him after his traumatic experiences.

Plot Overview:
    After Lord Ruthven's death, Aubrey is haunted by his memories and the oath he took to conceal Ruthven's crimes. He discovers that the corpse of the woman attacked by a vampire has vanished, leading him to suspect the involvement of robbers. Seeking to escape the horrors of his past, Aubrey travels to Smyrna and later returns to England, where he is consumed by guilt and fear over Ruthven's potential return. Aubrey's mental state deteriorates as he grapples with the implications of his oath and the threat Ruthven poses to his loved ones. Despite his guardians' attempts to help him, Aubrey becomes increasingly paranoid and incoherent, ultimately leading to a tragic confrontation on the eve of his sister's wedding to Lord Ruthven, who has manipulated events to ensure the marriage occurs. In a final act of desperation, Aubrey reveals the truth to his sister, but it is too late; she has already succumbed to Ruthven's influence.

    - The marriage is solemnized despite Aubrey's protests.
    - Aubrey's health declines rapidly after the wedding, leading to his death.
    - Miss Aubrey succumbs to Lord Ruthven's influence and becomes a vampire.
